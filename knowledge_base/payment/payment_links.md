## Summary

Stripe Payment Links enable businesses to accept payments with zero code. Users create a shareable link from the Stripe Dashboard and share it via social media, email, SMS, or website. Customers pay on a Stripe-hosted page that dynamically displays their local payment methods and currency. It is Stripe's simplest integration option (Complexity 1/5).

## Key Capabilities

- **No-code creation**: Create payment links directly from the Stripe Dashboard — no engineering required
- **Share anywhere**: Social media, email, SMS, QR codes, or embed as a buy button on any website
- **40+ payment methods**: Dynamically displayed based on customer location and eligibility
- **Adaptive Pricing**: Automatically shows local currency to each customer
- **30+ languages**: Matches the customer's browser language setting
- **Reusable links**: Use the same link for unlimited customers, or limit purchases per link
- **One-time & subscription payments**: Supports both payment types
- **Customer-chosen amounts**: Perfect for donations or pay-what-you-want models
- **Upsells & optional items**: Add additional products to the checkout flow
- **Promo codes**: Offer discount codes to customers
- **QR codes**: Generate QR codes for in-person sharing
- **UTM tracking**: Use URL parameters for campaign tracking and reconciliation
- **Automatic receipts**: Email receipts sent automatically to customers
- **No-code refunds**: Issue refunds directly from the Dashboard
- **PCI compliance**: Fully handled by Stripe — no compliance burden on the merchant

## Sales Use Case

Use this document when a customer asks about selling online without a website, accepting payments quickly for an event or product launch, adding payment to social media channels, accepting donations, or testing a product idea without building a full checkout integration.

## Client-ready Explanation

Stripe Payment Links is the fastest way to start selling online — no website or coding required. You create a link in your Stripe Dashboard, share it anywhere (email, SMS, social media, even a QR code), and customers pay on a Stripe-hosted page that automatically adapts to their language and preferred payment methods. It's ideal for creators, early-stage businesses, events, donations, or anyone who wants to start accepting payments in minutes rather than weeks.

## Key Discovery Questions

- Does the customer have a website, or do they need to sell purely through shared links?
- What are they selling (physical products, digital goods, services, donations, subscriptions)?
- Do they need customers to be able to choose their own payment amount?
- What channels do they plan to share links through (social media, email, QR codes)?
- Do they need upsells, promo codes, or limited-quantity purchases?
- If the customer needs more advanced customization or invoicing capabilities, consider whether Payment Links, Invoicing, or a full Checkout integration is the better fit

## Price

Payment Links are included at no extra charge with standard Stripe Payments processing (2.9% + $0.30 per successful domestic card charge). There are no monthly fees or setup costs for using Payment Links.

## Escalation Notes

- If the customer needs complex invoice customization, suggest evaluating Stripe Invoicing instead
- If the customer needs a fully branded checkout on their own domain, suggest Stripe Checkout
- For high-volume Payment Link usage, standard Stripe volume/custom pricing may apply
- If the customer asks about restricted or age-gated products, escalate for compliance review
