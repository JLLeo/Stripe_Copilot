## Summary

**INTERNAL ONLY — DO NOT SHARE WITH CUSTOMERS.** This document outlines mock internal guidelines for when and how Stripe sales representatives should offer custom pricing to customers. It establishes volume thresholds, discount ranges, approval workflows, and competitive response strategies.

## Key Capabilities (Internal Guidelines)

### Custom Pricing Qualification Thresholds
- **Annual volume > $1M**: Eligible for custom pricing discussion
- **Annual volume > $10M**: Priority custom pricing with dedicated deal desk support
- **Annual volume > $100M**: Enterprise pricing with bespoke contract terms
- **Multi-product adoption** (3+ products): Additional discount tier available
- **Platform/Connect businesses**: Separate pricing track with buy-rate considerations

### Discount Ranges (Internal Reference)
- **Standard volume discount**: 5-15% off published rates for $1M-$10M annual volume
- **Enterprise discount**: 15-25% off published rates for $10M-$100M annual volume
- **Strategic discount**: 25%+ for $100M+ with multi-year commitment and multi-product adoption
- **IC+ pricing markup**: Typically 0.15%-0.30% above interchange for qualified accounts
- **Platform buy-rates**: Negotiated per platform; typically 10-30% below standard rates

### Approval Workflow (Mock)
1. Sales rep identifies custom pricing opportunity and collects customer volume data
2. Sales rep submits pricing proposal via internal deal desk tool
3. Deal desk reviews within 48 hours (standard) or 24 hours (priority)
4. For discounts > 25%, VP of Sales approval required
5. For strategic deals ($100M+), CRO approval required
6. All custom pricing requires signed agreement with volume commitment

### Competitive Response Strategies (Mock)
- **vs. Adyen**: Emphasize Stripe's developer experience, documentation quality, and integrated product suite
- **vs. Braintree/PayPal**: Emphasize Stripe's modern API, reliability, and enterprise readiness
- **vs. Square**: Emphasize Stripe's API-first approach, global capabilities, and platform suitability
- **vs. Local processors**: Emphasize Stripe's global reach, unified dashboard, and innovation velocity
- **Competitive price matching**: Available only for accounts > $10M; requires competitor pricing documentation

### What NOT to Discount (Mock Policy)
- Stripe Tax pricing (regulatory product — fixed pricing)
- Stripe Atlas ($500 one-time — fixed)
- Hardware (Terminal readers — manufactured cost)
- Dispute fees ($15 — network pass-through)
- Connect platform fees below 0.25% markup floor

## Sales Use Case

This document should be retrieved when an internal sales rep is preparing for a pricing negotiation, evaluating whether a customer qualifies for custom pricing, or needs to understand approval workflows and discount boundaries.

## Client-ready Explanation

**DO NOT SHARE THIS CONTENT WITH CUSTOMERS.** When discussing custom pricing with customers, use only the following approved language:

> "Stripe offers custom pricing packages for businesses with significant payment volume or unique business models. This can include volume discounts, interchange-plus pricing, multi-product bundles, and country-specific rates. I'd be happy to connect you with our pricing team to explore what might be available for your specific situation. Could you share your approximate annual processing volume and any current pricing you're working with?"

## Key Discovery Questions (Internal Use)

- What is the customer's verified annual processing volume? (Request processor statements)
- What is their current processor and what rates are they paying?
- Which Stripe products are they committed to using?
- Are they willing to sign a volume commitment or multi-year agreement?
- What is the competitive landscape? Are they actively evaluating other processors?
- Is this a new business win or an existing customer renegotiation?
- What is the customer's LTV (Lifetime Value) potential?

## Price

**INTERNAL REFERENCE ONLY:**
- Minimum custom pricing threshold: $1M annual volume
- Standard IC+ markup range: 0.15%-0.30%
- Platform minimum markup: 0.25%
- Maximum standard discount without VP approval: 25%

## Escalation Notes

- All custom pricing discussions must go through the internal deal desk — never commit to rates verbally
- For discounts beyond standard ranges, escalate to VP of Sales with business case
- Competitive price matching requires documented competitor pricing (processor statement or written quote)
- Strategic deals ($100M+) require CRO-level engagement and board visibility
- Non-profit/education: separate discount program available — route to social impact team
- If a customer demands pricing below processing cost (interchange + assessments), flag as non-viable and escalate immediately
