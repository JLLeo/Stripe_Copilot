## Summary

Stripe operates on a pay-as-you-go pricing model with no setup fees, monthly fees, or hidden charges. The standard domestic card rate is 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. Stripe also offers custom pricing packages for businesses with large payment volumes or unique business models, including interchange-plus (IC+) pricing, volume discounts, multi-product discounts, and country-specific rates.

## Key Capabilities

### Standard Pricing
- **Domestic cards**: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction
- **International cards**: +1.5% surcharge
- **Manually entered cards**: +0.5% surcharge
- **Currency conversion**: +1% if conversion is required
- **ACH Direct Debit**: 0.8% (capped at $5.00)
- **Link Instant Bank Payments**: 2.6% + $0.30
- **BNPL (Klarna/Afterpay)**: 5.99% + $0.30
- **Stablecoins**: 1.5% of transaction
- **In-person (Terminal)**: 2.7% + $0.05
- **Refunds**: No additional fees (original processing fees not returned)

### Key Product Add-on Pricing
- **Radar for Fraud Teams**: $0.02/transaction
- **Billing (pay-as-you-go)**: 0.7% of Billing volume
- **Invoicing Starter**: 0.4% per paid invoice
- **Tax Complete**: From $90/month
- **Sigma**: From $10/month (annual)
- **Data Pipeline**: From $50/month (annual)
- **Revenue Recognition**: $25/month
- **Custom Domain (Checkout)**: $10/month
- **Terminal Hardware**: Reader M2 ($59), S710 ($299), S700 ($299)

### Custom Pricing Options
- **Interchange-plus (IC+) pricing**: Pay interchange fees directly plus a fixed Stripe markup
- **Volume discounts**: Tiered discounts based on processing volume commitments
- **Multi-product discounts**: Savings when using multiple Stripe products together
- **Country-specific rates**: Optimized rates for specific markets
- **Global single rates**: Optional aggregated pricing across all markets
- **Discounted flat rates**: Custom percentage + fixed fee structure
- **Platform buy-rates**: Discounted rates for Stripe Connect platforms
- **Cost transparency dashboards**: APIs and dashboards for tracking processing costs

## Sales Use Case

Use this document when a customer asks about Stripe's pricing, compares Stripe costs against other processors, wants to understand if they qualify for custom pricing, or needs a detailed breakdown of what they'd pay for their specific business model.

## Client-ready Explanation

Stripe's standard pricing is transparent and simple: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful card charge, with no setup or monthly fees. You only pay when you get paid. For businesses processing significant volume or with unique models, we offer custom pricing packages that can include interchange-plus pricing, volume discounts, multi-product bundles, and country-specific rates. Stripe doesn't lock you into long-term contracts on standard pricing — you can start today and explore custom options as you grow.

## Key Discovery Questions

- What is the customer's annual processing volume?
- What is their average transaction size?
- What is their business model (e-commerce, SaaS, marketplace, services)?
- What is their card mix (domestic vs. international, debit vs. credit)?
- Which Stripe products are they using or planning to use?
- Are they currently using another processor? What rates are they paying?
- Do they have interchange-plus pricing experience?
- What countries do they operate in?
- Are they willing to commit to volume tiers for better rates?

## Price

Standard pricing: 2.9% + $0.30 per domestic card transaction. Custom pricing available for qualified businesses. Full pricing details at [stripe.com/pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing).

## Escalation Notes

- **Custom pricing discussions must be escalated to Stripe sales** — do not quote specific custom rates without approval
- For customers processing $1M+/year, proactively escalate for custom pricing evaluation
- For customers with an IC+ pricing request, escalate to sales with current processor statements if available
- If a customer is comparing Stripe against a competitor's quoted rate, escalate with competitor details for competitive analysis
- **Important**: Never promise specific custom pricing rates to customers. All custom pricing requires Stripe sales team approval and onboarding.
- For non-profit or education customers, escalate for potential special pricing programs
