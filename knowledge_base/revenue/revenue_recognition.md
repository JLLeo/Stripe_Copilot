## Summary

Stripe Revenue Recognition automates accrual accounting by applying revenue recognition rules to Stripe transactions. It helps businesses comply with accounting standards (ASC 606, IFRS 15) by automating the process of recognizing revenue over time rather than at the point of payment. It provides custom rules, multi-currency support, reporting, and integration with your General Ledger.

## Key Capabilities

- **Automated revenue recognition**: Automatically determines when revenue should be recognized based on configurable rules — not just at payment time
- **Global accounting standards**: Built-in support for ASC 606 (US GAAP) and IFRS 15 (International)
- **Custom rules**: Configure rules to handle revenue treatments specific to your business model
- **Multi-currency support**: Handle presentment and settlement currencies in revenue recognition calculations
- **Reports & exports**: Generate revenue reports and export CSV summaries via the API
- **Audit trail**: Drill into revenue numbers from the Stripe Dashboard to examine transaction-level detail
- **Chart of Accounts mapping**: Map Stripe transactions to your General Ledger's chart of accounts
- **Data import**: Import data from other sources to manage all revenue recognition in one place
- **Connect support**: Revenue recognition for platforms — destination charges, direct charges, and separate charges & transfers
- **Data freshness controls**: Understand when data is available for different use cases

## Sales Use Case

Use this document when a customer is a publicly traded company or startup approaching IPO/audit, needs ASC 606 or IFRS 15 compliance, has complex revenue recognition requirements (multi-year contracts, milestone billing, bundled services), or wants to automate their month-end close process.

## Client-ready Explanation

Stripe Revenue Recognition automates one of the most complex parts of financial operations: determining when to recognize revenue. For subscription and recurring revenue businesses, cash received today often needs to be recognized over months or years under accounting rules like ASC 606 and IFRS 15. Stripe Revenue Recognition applies your business rules automatically, producing audit-ready revenue schedules, detailed reports, and a clean audit trail — so your finance team spends less time on manual spreadsheets and more time on strategic work.
